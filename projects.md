### Wealth and social cohesion

From 2025-2029, I am a principal investigator of the project "Wealth and Social Cohesion from a Relational Perspective" funded by the Volkswagen Foundation. The research project studies how widening wealth gaps are reshaping the foundations of social life across countries. 

Visit [https://wealthandcohesion.com/](https://wealthandcohesion.com/) to learn more about the team and current research activities.
